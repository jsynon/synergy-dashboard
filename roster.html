Guild activity policy

Inactivity limits

  • Initiate Rank 17 days
  • Wayfarer Rank 214 days
  • Stalwart Rank 330 days
  • Veteran Rank 460 days
  • Elder Rank 590 days
  • Officer Rank 6Exempt

Limits are evaluated at the account level using the most recent activity across its characters. Reaching a limit makes an account eligible for removal; it does not remove the account automatically.

Guild advancement

Rank Progression Benchmarks

  • Wayfarer Rank 2~1 month
  • Stalwart Rank 3~3 months
  • Veteran Rank 4~6 months
  • Elder Rank 5~12 months
  • Officer Rank 6By appointment only

These are general benchmarks based on the account’s earliest verified guild join date. Active participation can support faster progression. Rank decisions also consider activity, participation in guild events or runs, communication, respectful behavior, and positive contribution.
