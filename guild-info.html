Our Community

Guild Overview

Who we are, how we play, and what members can expect.

Synergy is a community-driven guild focused on teamwork, learning, and steady progression. We welcome players of all experience levels, from those still learning the game to seasoned endgame adventurers. Our goal is to foster an environment where members can grow, improve, and enjoy the game without pressure, gatekeeping, or toxicity.

We believe that strong groups are built through cooperation, communication, and shared understanding rather than rushing or skipping steps. Our approach emphasizes respectful, cooperative play; clear and intentional progression through content; and helping members learn roles, mechanics, and builds at a sustainable pace. Learning is encouraged, questions are welcome, and mistakes are treated as opportunities for growth rather than failures.

Structured Support and Progression

Synergy regularly organizes dungeon runs, training runs, and guild events designed to support both newer and experienced players. Communication and preparation are valued, but perfection is not expected. Endgame content, including master dungeons and trials, is fully supported, with guidance on gear progression, builds, and strategy. Participation is based on readiness and willingness to learn—not on already having best-in-slot gear. Progression is part of the journey, not a barrier to entry.

Community and Culture

As a community, Synergy values patience, accountability, and mutual support. Members are encouraged to contribute positively, respect one another’s time and effort, and help maintain a welcoming and inclusive atmosphere. We celebrate achievements together, provide mentorship for newer players, and share knowledge to help everyone reach their goals.

Learning and Growth

We provide structured opportunities for members to improve their skills, including class-specific guidance, build optimization, and strategy discussions. Our goal is to help members feel confident in their roles and to encourage collaboration and teamwork across all content. Mistakes are a natural part of learning, and curiosity and questions are always welcome.

Engagement and Participation

Whether you’re here to learn, improve, tackle challenging content, or help others along the way, you’ll find a place in Synergy as long as you engage in good faith and support the community. Active participation is encouraged, but everyone is free to contribute at their own pace. The guild thrives on mutual respect, shared effort, and a collective commitment to enjoying the game together.

Why Join Synergy?

  • Friendly, patient, and supportive community
  • Structured progression and guidance for all levels
  • Endgame content fully supported with strategy and builds
  • Opportunities to mentor others or learn from experienced players
  • Inclusive atmosphere where questions are encouraged and mistakes are part of learning
  • Regular guild events, runs, and collaborative activities

Community Standards

Guild Rules

Guidelines for respectful play, fair participation, and steady progression.

Be Polite

Politeness is the foundation of a healthy community. When you log in, take a moment to greet guild or alliance members who are online. A simple “hello” helps keep the environment warm and welcoming. Likewise, if someone greets you, please respond; even a brief acknowledgement goes a long way.

We have members from many different countries, cultures, and time zones. English is not the primary language for a large portion of our players, so it’s important to show patience and understanding. If someone misunderstands something or struggles to express themselves clearly, take the time to explain things calmly rather than getting frustrated. Remember that tone doesn’t always translate well in text, so aim to communicate clearly and respectfully at all times.

Helping Members

This guild operates on the idea that we all improve when we support each other. If you need help, whether it’s running content, learning a mechanic, building your character, or understanding a system, please ask. No question is too small or too obvious. Everyone was new once, and we’d rather you ask than struggle in silence.

Officers and leaders are often online throughout the day, spanning many time zones, but they may also be busy running content or helping others. If you need assistance, feel free to speak up in guild or alliance chat. Someone will usually respond relatively quickly.

If you need something from the guild bank, you are welcome to ask an officer or a guild leader. Requests should be reasonable, and high-value items may require approval. Everything in the bank exists to support guild members; don’t feel intimidated about asking.

No Drama

We want the guild to be a relaxing and enjoyable place. To maintain that, please avoid discussions that typically lead to hostility or arguments. This includes politics, religion, and controversial real-life events.

If you encounter a problem with another guild member, alliance member, or even a random player from outside the guild, do not escalate the situation yourself. Instead, reach out to an officer or guild leader and let us handle it. We can mediate, investigate, and step in when necessary.

Whenever possible, try to gather evidence, such as screenshots, so that issues can be addressed properly. Do not bring personal disagreements or disputes into alliance chat. Keep conflict private and let leadership take care of it. Our shared goal is to enjoy the game, protect the guild’s reputation, and maintain a calm space for everyone.

Keep It Civil

All interactions should remain respectful. Absolutely no slurs, name-calling, harassment, or abusive behavior will be accepted. A joke is fine until it becomes hurtful; if someone tells you a topic makes them uncomfortable, be considerate.

Mild swearing is generally okay. Most of us are adults, and sometimes emotions run high in difficult content. Still, keep things reasonable and avoid directing profanity at another person.

Do NOT Kick

Members are not allowed to kick guild or alliance players from a party or queue group except in very specific situations. A kick may only be issued if the person has clearly broken guild or alliance rules, and even then, only with evidence such as a screenshot.

If someone disconnects mid-run, do not assume they rage-quit or abandoned the group. Disconnects happen for many reasons: network issues, game crashes, system freezes, or real-life emergencies. Please wait at least five full minutes before considering a kick to give them time to return.

Single Account Policy

To ensure maximum opportunity for all members, we maintain a one-account-per-person limit on the roster. While you are welcome to have multiple characters within that single account in the guild, secondary or “storage” accounts are not permitted. This ensures that roster spots are held by unique individuals rather than occupied by the same player multiple times. Officers are exempt from this policy.

Main Account Residency

Synergy is a home for active, primary accounts. We do not provide roster space for alt characters if your main character is established in another guild. We expect your primary focus and participation to be here; if your main account resides elsewhere, we ask that you consolidate your characters to a single community to free up space for those dedicated to our progression.

Playing With a Significant Other

If you prefer to run content exclusively with your partner, please form your own party and ask others to join you. What you cannot do is join an existing run, participate for a bit, and then leave mid-content simply because your partner wasn’t there or didn’t get in.

Leaving mid-run disrupts everyone else’s time and effort, and it is disrespectful to the group. If you sign up for a run, commit to finishing it with the team that started it.

No Elitism

Our guild welcomes everyone, from fresh leveling players to veterans who have spent years in the endgame. Because of this, elitism in any form is not tolerated. This means:

  • No unreasonable item level requirements for guild or alliance activities.
  • No judging or excluding players based on their chosen class, build path, or gear progression.
  • No gatekeeping using vague or coded terms like “hdps,” “exp,” or anything designed to exclude less experienced players.
  • No creating restrictive “invisible rules” for who can join queues or events.

Everyone started with low item level and limited knowledge at some point. Give players time to learn and grow. Every build and class path is viable in scaled content, and people should feel free to play the way they enjoy.

About Master Content

Older Master content (anything two modules old or older) should be open to everyone. These activities are designed so that any player can participate with support and teamwork. The only exception is current Master content, which may reasonably require certain item levels, stats, or items. These requirements exist because the difficulty level demands it, not because of elitism.

We use a first-come, first-served system for invitations: if someone sends a “+”, they are invited. No exceptions, no favoritism. Requesting the normal item level needed to enter random queues, private queues, or fulfill basic game roles (tank/heals/DPS) is completely acceptable and is not considered elitist.

Guild Rank Progression Policy

Guild ranks are based on time in the guild, activity, and participation. There are general time benchmarks for rank progression, but active participation can lead to faster promotions.

General Guidelines

  • Rank 2: ~1 month in guild
  • Rank 3: ~3 months in guild
  • Rank 4: ~6 months in guild
  • Rank 5: ~12 months in guild
  • Rank 6: By appointment only

In addition to time, rank progression considers:

  • Regular in-game activity
  • Participation in guild events or runs
  • Communication and presence in Discord
  • Respectful behavior and positive contribution to the community

Ranks exist to reflect trust, reliability, and engagement, not item level, DPS, or favoritism. Activity and attitude matter just as much as time served. If you believe you meet the criteria for a rank update or feel you’ve been overlooked, you’re welcome to contact an officer. Transparency and fairness matter to us.

Guild Inactivity Policy

To keep the roster current, accounts may be reviewed for removal after the following period without any character on the account logging in:

  • Rank 1 — Initiate: 7 days
  • Rank 2 — Wayfarer: 14 days
  • Rank 3 — Stalwart: 30 days
  • Rank 4 — Veteran: 60 days
  • Rank 5 — Elder: 90 days
  • Rank 6 — Officer: Exempt

Inactivity is evaluated by account, using the most recent login among its characters. Logging in on any character on the account resets the inactivity period. Reaching a threshold makes an account eligible for removal; it does not trigger automatic removal. Our Principles

The Synergy Guild Charter

The values and expectations that guide our community.

Synergy is a cooperative Neverwinter guild built on patience, learning, and shared progression. We believe the best endgame experiences come from teamwork, communication, and mutual respect—not rushing, elitism, or silent carries.

What We Value

  • Growth over perfection — mistakes are part of learning.
  • Teamwork over ego — no role is more important than the group.
  • Respect and kindness — toward guildmates, alliance members, and PUGs alike.
  • Teaching and learning — knowledge is meant to be shared.

Our Approach to Progression

Progression in Synergy is earned, not skipped. We recognize a natural path:

Campaign → Advanced Dungeons → Master Content → Trials

Members are encouraged to ask questions, seek advice on builds and stats, and learn mechanics rather than being carried through them. Wipes happen. They’re expected. They’re how we improve.

How We Treat Each Other

  • Toxicity, harassment, or elitism have no place here.
  • Feedback should be constructive, never demeaning.
  • No one is pressured to play content they aren’t ready for.
  • No one is left behind without explanation.

If something feels off, talk to an officer. Silence helps no one.

What We Ask of Members

  • Be honest about your experience level.
  • Be willing to learn and help others learn.
  • Communicate—especially in group content.
  • Represent Synergy with respect in-game and in alliance spaces.

You don’t need to be the best player. You do need to be a good guildmate.

Our Story

Guild History

From BAMF to Synergy, and the chapters that shaped the guild.

BAMF and Lady Vega

Before Synergy, there was BAMF—a power both singular and manifold. Forged by the vision of Lady Vega, BAMF was at once a guild and an alliance, a living banner beneath which many paths converged. In its earliest days, BAMF was not merely a gathering of adventurers; it was a crucible of ambition and fellowship, a forge where the strength of individuals was tempered into collective might. Warriors, mages, rogues, and strategists alike found a place beneath its standard, drawn to the promise of purpose and the light of Lady Vega’s leadership.

Under her hand, BAMF became a nexus—a place where vision met action, and where ambition was united by shared resolve. Each member, each faction, each adventurer who pledged loyalty added a thread to the tapestry, weaving together disparate skills, dreams, and destinies. In this time, BAMF was expansion incarnate: bold, unstoppable, and radiant with potential.

Fracture and Renewal

But no structure, no matter how meticulously forged, escapes the inexorable passage of time. When Lady Vega departed, the bonds that held the wider alliance began to strain. The unity that had once seemed unshakable now wavered, as diverging ambitions and differing paths pulled the members in opposite directions. The alliance that bore the BAMF name fractured first, leaving echoes of former strength scattered across the lands. What remained, however, was the guild itself—a core of steadfast members, standing at a crossroads, uncertain if their future would be defined by survival or dissolution.

Yet BAMF was not a force to fade quietly. Refusing to be defined by the fracture of the alliance it had once nurtured, the guild chose separation over decline. It stepped away from the wider network it had helped to shape, leaving the remnants to be carried forward under Occasum’s guidance. That splintered alliance, forged anew from the pieces of what had been, would in time adopt a new name—Sol Mortis—a symbol of endings, transformation, and the inevitability of change.

Freed from its former mantle, BAMF sought a new constellation, a place where its strength could endure and grow. That place was found within the Wanted Alliance—a new network, new allies, and a new horizon. There, BAMF endured, tempered by adversity and strengthened through trials. What had been tested by loss was honed into resilience. The guild was no longer defined solely by legacy or past triumphs, but by deliberate choice and purposeful action. Its members carried with them the lessons of alliance, collapse, and rebirth, knowing that true strength arises not from conquest alone, but from the courage to endure and to unify.

The Emergence of Synergy

From this crucible of experience, Synergy emerged. Synergy did not rise as a mere continuation of BAMF’s flame; it rose as its refinement. Where BAMF had been expansion and fire, Synergy became alignment and balance. Where BAMF had pursued breadth and ambition, Synergy pursued harmony and intention. It carried forward the wisdom of collapse, choosing cooperation over scale, purpose over prestige, and understanding over mere strength.

In Synergy, the echoes of the past are honored, but not allowed to define the present. Each member contributes as both individual and part of a greater whole, bound by respect and sustained by shared vision. Every voice carries weight; every action shapes the guild’s destiny. What remains is not the shadow of old power, nor a hollow echo of former glory—it is the distilled essence of experience, a guild forged by trial and tempered by purpose, stronger for the lessons of its past.

The story of Synergy is still being written. It is not chronicled only in victories or the banners won on the battlefield, but in the enduring patience of its members, the quiet strength of unity, and the deliberate care with which every choice is made. It is a living testament to what emerges when ambition is guided by wisdom, when power is balanced with respect, and when many voices act in concert as one. In this, its legend continues, and the tapestry of its story grows ever richer, thread by deliberate thread.

Academy and Forge

And so the guild’s saga continued, carving its path through dungeons and trials, ever growing in skill and renown. From the quiet fires of our earlier campaigns emerged a new chapter: Synergy Academy. Born from the guild’s desire to forge champions of progression, the Academy became a crucible where experience met guidance, where veterans shared their wisdom and newcomers honed their craft. Here, the flame of learning burned bright, guided by our dedicated organizers, who marshaled trials, mentored new members, and ensured every clear strengthened the guild. Through their efforts, the Academy became a crucible of both skill and unity,

As the Academy grew, so too did our ranks, and whispers of the past stirred anew. From the shadows of memory, Vega, the founder of what Synergy once was before it became the mighty Synergy, returned to our halls. Her presence reminded all of us that every legend has roots, and every guild, a past that shapes its future. With Vega among us once more, the guild felt the weight and inspiration of history—a bridge between who we were and what we were becoming.

Through trials, triumphs, and endless nights of grinding, Synergy pressed onward, guided by the wisdom of the old and the hunger of the new. The Academy thrived. New legends were forged. And the guild’s tale, ever unbroken, moved boldly toward the next chapter.

In time, as Synergy’s foundation grew ever stronger, a new pillar of the guild emerged—one not forged in battle alone, but in patience, knowledge, and craft. From the collective ambition of its members arose the Forge, a mastercrafting initiative dedicated to the creation of powerful tools, resources, and opportunities for all. The Forge was more than a section of the guild; it was a testament to Synergy’s evolving identity. Where once strength had been measured primarily in arms and conquest, it now found equal expression in creation and cooperation.

Master artisans, gatherers, and dedicated contributors came together, pooling their efforts to refine materials, share knowledge, and elevate one another. Guided by the same principles that shaped the Academy, the Forge became a place of mentorship as much as production. Veterans passed down their expertise, newcomers found their footing, and each contribution—no matter how small—was recognized as part of a greater whole. The act of crafting became, in itself, an extension of Synergy’s philosophy: that true strength is not hoarded, but built together.

Through the Forge, the guild’s reach extended beyond the battlefield, ensuring that its members were not only prepared for the trials ahead, but empowered to shape them. It stood as a symbol of refinement—of raw potential transformed through effort and unity into something enduring. And so Synergy continues to grow—not only as warriors, but as builders. Not only as individuals, but as a collective bound by purpose. With the Academy guiding its future and the Forge strengthening its foundation, the guild moves forward, ever shaping its legacy—tempered by experience, united in vision, and steadfast in the belief that what is built together will endure.
